## Template (Design section, "use for all pages")
`design/Template.png` = screenshot of a video of an open **green ring binder** spread: 3 metal rings down the centre spine; coloured index tabs sticking out on the left edge (blue, pink, yellow) and on the right edge (green, orange, red, with vertical labels such as "FOR RETAIL", "THE TEAM"); small running header text top-left/top-right of each page and a small footer bar. Each page is a green sheet with black image slots (green gutters):
- Left page: full-width wide panel on top; a row of 4 equal small squares; a large panel; a full-width short strip at the bottom.
- Right page: two stacked full-width panels on top; then a 2×2 grid of equal cells.
The current site's Design section (`js/design.js`, ring binder with divider tabs, pages coloured by section) already implements the binder; the requested change is to lay each page's images out in these Template slot grids.
